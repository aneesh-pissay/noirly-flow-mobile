Noirly Flow keeps your work in one calm place: an inbox for what's next, boards for how it's moving, and a calendar for when it's due. Use it on your own, or bring your team and see changes the moment they happen.

PLAN YOUR DAY
• An inbox for everything that isn't in a project yet
• Quick add with priority and due date in one line
• Filter by status, priority, due date or "assigned to me"
• Tap to complete, swipe to delete, long-press for quick edits

RUN YOUR PROJECTS
• List, board and calendar views for every project
• Drag cards between To do, In progress and Done
• Month calendar with tasks per day, plus everything still unscheduled

EVERY DETAIL IN THE TASK
• Description, status, priority, due date and repeats (daily or weekly)
• Checklists and subtasks for the smaller steps
• Tags and assignees so everyone knows what's theirs
• Comments with names, and the full history of each task

WORK AS A TEAM
• Personal and team workspaces, switch in a tap
• Roles for owners, admins, members and viewers
• Invite people with a link, or share it from your phone
• See who's online and watch the board update live
• An activity feed of every change, exportable as CSV

FIND ANYTHING
• Search tasks and projects, or create one straight from what you typed

ONE NOIRLY ACCOUNT
Sign in with email or Google. The same account works across Noirly apps and on the web at noirly.flow.aneesh-pissay.in.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
No ads and no trackers. Traffic is encrypted, and you can delete your account and your data at any time from Menu → Delete account.
